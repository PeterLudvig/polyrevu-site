For site owners

Our crawler

PolyRevu is building a guide to restaurants and other places to eat and drink, based on what people have publicly written about them. The site is not yet open to the public. This page says what our crawler reads, how it behaves, and how to turn it away.

Identification

How to recognise it

If you run a website, you may see requests from PolyRevuHonestFetchBot. Its full identification is:

PolyRevuHonestFetchBot/1.0 (+https://polyrevu.com/about/crawler-notice; contact: data@polyrevu.com)

It always identifies itself with this name. It never pretends to be a browser or another crawler.

What it reads

What it does

  • It reads individual public pages that discuss a specific restaurant or venue: for example an article, a guide, or the venue's own website and menu.
  • From a page it records short structured notes: which venue, what was discussed, and whether the remark was favourable or not.
  • It does not keep copies of your pages or your text, and it does not republish your writing.

Manners

How it behaves

  • It reads and obeys robots.txt, including Crawl-delay.
  • It waits at least two seconds between requests to the same site and fetches only a small number of pages per site.
  • It does not log in, does not go behind paywalls, and does not submit forms.

Opt out

How to turn it away

To stop our crawler from reading your site, add this to your robots.txt:

User-agent: PolyRevuHonestFetchBot
Disallow: /

You can also write to us and we will stop fetching your site and remove what we derived from it.

Questions or concerns

Questions, concerns, or a request to stop: data@polyrevu.com. Please include your domain name.
